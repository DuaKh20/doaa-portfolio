Demand Forecasting Dashboard

3 views

Actual units sold against the 35-week forecast, with demand by category and location.

Base forecast against the signal-adjusted forecast, with each driver's contribution.

Adjusted revenue forecast against the baseline, by category and by location.

Problem
Plan demand and revenue for 12 SKUs sold online and through branches in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam.
Approach
A 35-week forecast set against a baseline, adjusted for external signals (Ramadan, Eid and other events, social media trend, weather) and converted into revenue.
Outcome
The adjusted forecast reaches 530K units against a 505K baseline (+5.02%), a SAR 258K revenue opportunity (1.27%). The external signals are simulated for demonstration.
